Everything you need to know before your visit, so there are no surprises.

Cancellation policy

We kindly ask for a minimum of 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule. This lets us offer the time to another guest. Late cancellations may be subject to a fee at the stylist's discretion.

Rescheduling policy

Need to move your appointment? No problem — just let us know at least 24 hours ahead and we'll happily find you a new time.

No-show policy

Guests who miss an appointment without notice may be asked for a deposit to secure future bookings.

Late-arrival policy

If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, we may need to shorten or reschedule your service so we can stay on time for other guests.

Deposits

A deposit may be requested for longer colour services, bridal bookings or first-time guests. Any deposit is applied to your final bill.

Refunds & adjustments

Your happiness matters to us. If something isn't quite right, let us know within 7 days and we'll gladly arrange a complimentary adjustment.

Product returns

Unopened retail products may be returned within 14 days with your receipt. Opened products can only be returned if faulty.

Children & guests

We love welcoming families. For everyone's safety, we ask that children are supervised at all times and that extra guests are kept to a minimum in the styling area.

Allergies & patch testing

Please tell us about any allergies or sensitivities when booking. We offer patch testing before colour services and recommend it for anyone new to a colour product.
