Getting here

Parking: Paid street parking on Ossington Avenue plus a nearby Green P lot.

Transit: A 5-minute walk from Ossington Station; the 63 Ossington bus stops close by.

Accessibility: Ground-floor, step-free entrance and an accessible washroom. Let us know if we can make your visit more comfortable.

Cancellation & late arrivals

We kindly ask for at least 24 hours' notice to change or cancel an appointment. Arrivals more than 15 minutes late may need to be rescheduled out of fairness to other guests. Full details are on our Policies page.
