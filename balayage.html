The service

Effortless, lived-in colour

Balayage is a freehand colouring technique where colour is hand-painted onto the hair for a natural, gradual lightening effect. The result is dimensional, low-maintenance colour with no harsh regrowth line — perfect if you want a brighter look without frequent salon visits.

Who it's for

Balayage suits almost everyone, from subtle warmth on darker hair to bright, beachy blondes. It's especially popular with guests who want a natural finish and a relaxed maintenance routine.

What to expect

  • A consultation to plan tone, placement and hair health
  • Hand-painted lightening tailored to your features
  • A toner or gloss to perfect the final shade
  • A bond-building treatment and style to finish

Preparation & aftercare

Come with clean, dry, product-free hair, and bring a few inspiration photos. Afterwards, use colour-safe, sulphate-free products, wash in cooler water and book a gloss every few months to keep your tone looking fresh.

Balayage FAQs

Common questions

How long does balayage take?

Most balayage appointments take between two and three hours depending on your hair length, thickness and the level of lift you're after. We'll give you a clear time estimate at your consultation.

How much does balayage cost in Toronto?

Balayage at Maple & Rose starts at $180 CAD. Your final price depends on hair length, density and whether a toner or treatment is added — we confirm everything before we begin.

How often do I need to come back?

One of the joys of balayage is how gracefully it grows out. Most guests refresh every three to four months, with an optional gloss in between to keep tones fresh.

Will balayage damage my hair?

We prioritise hair health at every step, using bond-building products and only lifting as much as your hair can safely handle. A consultation helps us plan a result that keeps your hair in great condition.
