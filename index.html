Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to book in advance?

We recommend booking ahead, especially for colour and weekend appointments. You can request a time through our online form or give us a call — walk-ins are welcome when a chair is free.

What are your prices?

Every service lists a starting price in Canadian dollars on our Services page. Final pricing depends on hair length, thickness, product use and stylist level, which we always confirm during your consultation.

Do you offer a consultation before colour?

Yes. Every colour service begins with a consultation, and we're happy to book a separate no-obligation consultation for bigger changes or colour corrections.

Is there a new-client offer?

New guests receive 15% off their first cut or colour service. Just mention it when you book — see the offer section for details.

Where can I park?

There is paid street parking along Ossington Avenue and a Green P lot a short walk away. We're also a five-minute walk from the Ossington TTC station on the 63 bus route.
